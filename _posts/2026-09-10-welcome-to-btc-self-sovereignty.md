Welcome to the BTC Self-Sovereignty project! This is our first post on the journey towards promoting Bitcoin self-sovereignty principles and peer-to-peer technologies.

## What We're About

At BTC Self-Sovereignty, we believe in:

- **Decentralization**: Taking control of your own financial destiny
- **Peer-to-Peer**: Direct interactions without intermediaries
- **Bitcoin**: The foundation of digital money freedom
- **Education**: Sharing knowledge about self-sovereign systems

## Why This Matters

In a world of increasing financial surveillance and control, Bitcoin and peer-to-peer technologies offer an alternative path. Self-sovereignty means:

- Owning your private keys
- Running your own node
- Transacting without permission
- Maintaining financial privacy
